Tintolume Support

Color by number for iPhone and iPad

Contact us

Have a question or found a problem? Email momsy.app.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened.

Restore a purchase

Open the More tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Cancel a subscription

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Tintolume. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

Request a refund

Payments are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Unlock a picture

Pictures with a crown are part of Premium. You can subscribe to unlock all of them, or watch a short video to unlock one picture.

Progress on another device

Your progress syncs through iCloud when you are signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices and iCloud is turned on.
