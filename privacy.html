Tintolume Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Tintolume is a coloring app. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. The developer does not collect, see or sell your personal data. This page explains the few places where data leaves your device.

Your pictures and progress

Your coloring progress, albums and statistics are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also synced through Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud database. That database belongs to your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it. You can delete it in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Photos and Image Playground

If you turn one of your photos or an Image Playground image into a coloring picture, the conversion happens entirely on your device. The image is never uploaded. When you save a finished picture or wallpaper, Tintolume asks for permission to add it to your Photos library; it cannot read your library.

Purchases

Tintolume Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app only receives confirmation that a subscription is active. The developer never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel the subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Advertising

If you do not have Premium, Tintolume shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To deliver and measure ads, Unity may collect device information (such as device model, operating system, an app-specific device identifier, IP address and the approximate location derived from it) and how you interact with ads. Tintolume does not ask for permission to track you, does not access the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and requests non-personalized ads. Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Premium removes all ads.

Picture downloads

New pictures may be downloaded from a content server. These requests contain no personal data; like any web request, they reveal your IP address to the server, which does not store or use it to identify you.

Children

Tintolume is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
